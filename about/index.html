My philosophy

Exhale

I believe that conventional wisdom is fixated on the ideas of moving big, moving fast and moving constantly. And it's so easy to absorb the message that that's what we should be doing, even when it doesn't feel right.

This focus on speed and movement leaves little space for slow, deep thinking. So those of us who want to take our time are left feeling overwhelmed, mismatched and depleted.

When your brain feels like it's got 57 tabs open and there's just too much going on at the same time it's hard to know what feels right and to know how you want to move forward.

That's why I love to create spaces that are a haven from the brash environments we normally find ourselves in so you can exhale and feel free to be who and how you are.

Who I work with

My Clients

I work with women who know they're smart and capable, but have had dreams for a long time that they just haven't been able to make happen. And the longer those dreams have been on hold, the more they've wondered if there's something wrong with them.

They could be running a business, though they might not call themselves a high achiever, and definitely not a boss babe. They could be working a 9–5 and either looking for more fulfilment at work or dreaming of starting something of their own. Or they might have had an idea in a notebook for years that they've never told anyone about: a book they want to write, a move to another country, a completely different way of living.

The women I work best with come from all walks of life, but they all have one thing in common: their mind is so full of shoulds and doubt, and of trying to make themselves and their goals look acceptable to other people, that they've lost touch with what they really want.

The essence of what I do is provide a haven from the hurly-burly of everyday life, and slow everything down so they can feel calm in their body and in their mind. From that sense of ease, they're able to access something deep inside of them that can't be heard or felt when the mind isn't free to pay it attention.
