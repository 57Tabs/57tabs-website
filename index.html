Getting it done without setting your nervous system on fire.

When your head feels full to bursting, you’re circling the same decision, or doubting yourself, you don’t need an action plan, more effort or someone just telling you what to do. You need a space away from the outside world and all the noise, so you can hear your own voice.

When your mind settles you will know your one next step.

What you want

Some calm.

An end to the constant mental merry-go-round.

You want to focus on what matters and stop being distracted by the things you think you could or should be doing instead.

Most of all, you want to feel like you’re driving the bus again, instead of being held hostage on the express route with no stops for 20 miles.

Nice to meet you

Hi, I'm Laura.

Life Coach & Highly Sensitive Woman

My passions in life are Depeche Mode, The Thursday Murder Club and Hanoverian History. And helping other quiet, sensitive women make sense of their chaotic thinking.

This is the part where I’m supposed to sound smart and professional and like I’ve got it all together. The truth is my brain is just as chaotic most days as it ever was. In fact, probably more so, now we’re all plugged into the matrix, and the pressure to be constantly on drains my battery faster than a teenager playing Fortnite on their phone.

I hate being told what to do but I also have a fear of what other people think. It kept me stuck for years but I found my way out of the maze. If that sounds familiar you’ve come to the right place.
